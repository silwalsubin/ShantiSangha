# ShantiSangha

## Overview

ShantiSangha is a wellness and emotional support app designed to help people feel more grounded, supported, and understood in their everyday lives.

It is built for moments when someone wants a calm space to reflect, process emotions, check in with themselves, and receive supportive guidance without judgment.

ShantiSangha is not meant to replace a licensed therapist or emergency support. Its purpose is to provide everyday emotional support, reflection, and wellness guidance in a way that feels accessible and human.

## The Problem It Solves

Many people go through stress, anxiety, loneliness, emotional overwhelm, or mental fatigue without having immediate access to support.

Common challenges include:

- not having someone to talk to in the moment
- feeling emotionally overwhelmed and not knowing where to start
- struggling to process thoughts or feelings clearly
- wanting support between therapy sessions or before seeking therapy
- needing a private, low-pressure space for reflection
- finding it hard to build healthy emotional habits consistently

ShantiSangha helps by giving users a safe and supportive space for emotional check-ins, guided reflection, and wellness-focused conversations.

## Goals

The goal of ShantiSangha is to help users:

- feel supported during emotionally difficult moments
- better understand their thoughts and feelings
- build regular self-reflection habits
- improve emotional awareness over time
- access simple coping support when they feel stressed or stuck
- create a sense of calm, clarity, and personal stability

## Who It Is For

ShantiSangha is for people who want:

- emotional support in daily life
- a wellness companion for reflection and self-check-ins
- help organizing thoughts and feelings
- support building mindfulness and coping habits
- a gentle starting point before seeking deeper care

## Core User Experience

The product experience centers around giving users a calm, supportive, and non-judgmental place to return to whenever they need emotional grounding.

Users should feel like they have:

- a private space to reflect
- a consistent wellness companion
- guidance that feels calm and supportive
- helpful structure when emotions feel difficult to untangle

## Core Features

### Emotional Support Conversations

Users can chat with ShantiSangha for supportive, wellness-focused conversations that help them process feelings, reflect on their day, and feel less alone.

### Guided Reflection

The app helps users slow down and reflect through thoughtful prompts, journaling guidance, and structured emotional check-ins.

### Journaling

Users can write about their thoughts, moods, and daily experiences, creating a personal space for emotional expression and self-awareness.

### Mood Check-Ins

Simple mood check-ins help users notice patterns in how they feel and build awareness of their emotional state over time.

### Coping Exercises

The app offers supportive exercises for moments of stress, overwhelm, or emotional fatigue, helping users pause, reset, and recenter.

### Conversation Summaries

Users can receive summaries of what they explored, helping them keep track of insights, recurring themes, and next steps for self-care.

### Saved Insights

Important reflections, patterns, and personal takeaways can be saved so users can revisit what matters most to them.

### Voice Journaling

Users can speak their thoughts out loud when writing feels difficult, making emotional reflection more accessible and natural.

### Safety and Support Resources

If a user appears to be in distress, the product can guide them toward appropriate support resources and safer next steps.

## MVP Feature Set

The first version of ShantiSangha should focus on:

- onboarding with clear product boundaries and supportive framing
- chat-based emotional support and reflection
- journaling
- mood check-ins
- coping exercises
- conversation summaries
- saved insights
- basic voice journaling
- safety and support resource guidance

## Future Product Opportunities

After the MVP, ShantiSangha can expand into:

- deeper personalization
- smarter memory of user preferences and patterns
- more advanced voice experiences
- goals and habit support
- richer emotional insights over time
- optional connection to human support or professional care pathways

## Product Vision

ShantiSangha aims to become a trusted wellness companion for emotional support, reflection, and everyday mental well-being.

The long-term vision is to create a product that helps people feel less alone, more self-aware, and more emotionally supported in their daily lives.
