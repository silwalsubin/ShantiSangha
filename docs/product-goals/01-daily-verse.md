# 01 — Daily Verse on Home

## Priority: Highest
## Phase: 1 — Make What Exists Feel Complete
## Tab: Home

## The Problem

Home opens straight to goals and check-ins. It feels like a task manager, not a sacred space. There is no spiritual grounding before action.

## What to Build

A single daily verse displayed at the top of the Home tab, above goals.

- One verse per day, rotating from Gita, Dhammapada, Upanishads
- No "see more" button, no verse library, no bookmarking
- Just today's wisdom, then your practice below it

## Why This Matters

The verse is the difference between "open app, check boxes" and "open app, receive wisdom, then engage with your practice." It sets the tone. It gives the user a reason to open the app even on days they don't want to do anything.

## Scope

- Backend: seed a verses table or JSON file with ~365 verses, serve one per day
- Frontend: one component at the top of Home, styled in the sacred aesthetic
- No user interaction beyond reading

## What This Replaces

Nothing. This fills a gap that has existed since mood check-ins were removed.

## Definition of Done

User opens the app and sees a verse before they see their goals.
